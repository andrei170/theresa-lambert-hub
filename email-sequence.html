Email 2 · Day 2 · Achieve With Grace Methodology Preview

Subject: The discipline most founders skip.

Body:

Hi [first name],

The book I wrote in 2020 is called Achieve with Grace: A guide to elegance and effectiveness in intense workplaces.

The reason I called it that, instead of something louder, is that the founders who scale without breaking the people around them are the ones who operate with discipline that looks calm from the outside.

That calm is not personality. It is a cadence.

It is the Monday meeting that runs in 40 minutes instead of 90. It is the difficult conversation had on Tuesday instead of avoided until Friday. It is the quarter closed inside plan because the operating rhythm carried it there, not because the founder pushed harder for the last two weeks.

Most founders skip the cadence and try to scale through effort. The effort works for a season. Then the team starts to fray.

What I help founders install is the cadence underneath the effort. So the business carries itself, and the people inside it stay.

Email 5 · Day 8 · Founder Story Deep Cut

Subject: The quarter the team almost broke.

Body:

Hi [first name],

There is a quarter from the hotel-scaling years I have written about exactly once, in a Brainz column, and almost never spoken about.

We grew from 90 to 130 people inside ten weeks.

The market demanded it. The property could carry it. The systems and the operating cadence could not.

I watched three senior people, the kind of staff who hold the culture together, decide to leave because the rhythm we had built no longer matched the rhythm the business now required. They were right to leave. I was wrong to have let the cadence lag.

The lesson I took out of that quarter is the one I now lead every Mastermind cohort with.

You do not scale the business by adding people. You scale the cadence first, then add the people who fit the new cadence.

Otherwise, the people you started with will not finish the climb.

This is the deeper layer of what Achieve with Grace is about. The book is structured around it, the Mastermind is structured around it, and the Fractional COO consulting engagement opens with it.

Tomorrow I will share where you can hear me unpack the operator side of this in long-form.

With grace,

Theresa
