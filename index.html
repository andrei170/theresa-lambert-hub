Cost of Waiting

~$3.5-13K/mo in warm-audience capture lost across the book and the podcast. Book buyers typically convert to high-ticket coaching at 2-5% with a competent nurture sequence. Podcast listeners convert at 0.5-1% with episode-mapped routing. ~$40K-$160K Year-1 floor on the warm-audience funnel install. 6 months from now ·~$20K-$80K in warm-audience inventory walked past, plus another six months of book sales and podcast downloads that built brand awareness instead of feeding the funnel.

(2) Podcast funnel: each episode published with a companion landing page + 8-touch nurture sequence pulling from the book and the episode catalogue + same tier-routed CTA. The book is written, the episodes exist, only the email sequences and landing pages need building.
