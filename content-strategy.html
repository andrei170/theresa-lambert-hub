Phase 3

Content Strategy · Theresa Lambert

Prepared 2026-05-11 by AG1 Consulting. Built to support the consolidated brand (Bottleneck 1) + visible offer ladder (Bottleneck 2) + flagship VSL (Bottleneck 3) + LinkedIn ghostwriting cadence (Bottleneck 4).

The through-line for every piece: the Achieve with Grace book voice. Hospitality-elegance, structured authority, calm under pressure. Theresa is the operator who scaled a boutique hotel to 8-figures at the #1 ski resort in North America, every content asset is a footprint of that operator credibility.

Content Pillars

Three pillars, each tied to a stage in the offer ladder.

Pillar 1 · Achieve With Grace (Entry-Tier Authority)

The book voice as a content pillar. Posts, podcast episodes, LinkedIn essays, and YouTube long-forms about how to operate effectively in intense workplaces.

  • Hospitality-grade leadership cadence
  • Grace under pressure (the difficult conversation, the missed quarter, the team transition)
  • Female-founder identity in high-stakes operating environments
  • The structured Monday, the disciplined Friday, the calm Tuesday

Lands: entry-tier audience (cold prospects discovering Theresa). Funnels into The Way to $100K / signature masterclass.

Pillar 2 · Six-Figure Coach Diaries (Mid-Tier Authority)

The "Diaries of a Six Figure Coach" podcast register. Operator-to-operator transparency about scaling a service-based business past six figures.

  • The first 100K, what actually moved the needle
  • Pricing the way clients will pay
  • Hiring before you're ready, firing when you should
  • The week the cohort almost didn't launch and how it shipped anyway

Lands: mid-tier audience (Mastermind candidates). Funnels into Your First 100k Mastermind / LAUNCHED.

Pillar 3 · Fractional COO Field Notes (High-Tier Authority)

The Fractional COO + Systems register. Case-study breakdowns from the 8-fig hotel scaling era + B2B operator insights.

  • Scaling a team from 90 to 180+ without breaking the culture
  • The operating cadence that runs a $10M+ property
  • The systems audit that finds 18% of recoverable margin
  • The decision-tree for "do I hire a COO or stay founder-led?"

Lands: high-tier audience (1:1 ASCENT + Fractional COO Consulting candidates). Funnels into application-only offers.

VSL Plan

Hero VSL · Consolidated Homepage (4-7 min)

Beats:

  1. Cold-open hook (0:00-0:30), the asymmetric proof. "Before I called myself a coach or a consultant, I scaled a boutique hotel to 8-figures at the #1 ski resort in North America."
  2. Operator credibility (0:30-1:30), the team-led-from-90-to-180 numbers, the operating cadence she ran, the constraints.
  3. Methodology bridge (1:30-3:30), Achieve with Grace as the through-line. How hospitality-grade operating discipline applies to coaches and service-based founders.
  4. The two tracks (3:30-5:00), "That's why I built this for two kinds of operators." Coaching track (mentorship + signature programs). Consulting track (Fractional COO + Systems). One founder, two clearly-positioned tracks under one roof.
  5. CTA (5:00-end), invitation, not pressure. Direct to entry-tier offer or diagnostic quiz routing.

Register: hospitality-elegance professionalism. Structured, calm, articulate. The opposite of yelling-at-camera. Cormorant Garamond serif overlays. Soft accent-teal lower thirds.

Program-Specific VSLs (2-3 min each)

  • Mastermind VSL (Your First 100k Mastermind), beats: who it's for, what gets installed in the 12 weeks, who graduates, CTA.
  • ASCENT VSL (1:1 high-ticket), beats: ICP fit, the depth of the engagement, the founder-led work, application CTA.
  • Fractional COO VSL (consulting track), beats: B2B operator fit, the systems install timeline, ICP business stage, application CTA.

Day-1 install: recut hero footage into the program-specific versions as placeholder. Day 14-21: re-shoot bespoke program VSLs.

LinkedIn Ghostwriting Cadence (Bottleneck 4)

Frequency: 4-5x/week. Mix of post types.

Post-Type Mix

  • Tuesday, Operator essay (Pillar 1 or 3). Long-form (200-400 words). One operating principle, illustrated with a hotel-scaling story or a current Fractional COO engagement insight.
  • Wednesday, Founder transparency (Pillar 2). Mid-length. Behind-the-scenes from the Six Figure Coach Diaries register. Sometimes a podcast-clip companion.
  • Thursday, Strategic frame (Pillar 1). A Achieve with Grace concept reframed for current LinkedIn audience. Short and structured.
  • Friday, Soft CTA (any pillar). Invitation to the entry-tier offer / podcast episode / lead magnet. Never hard-sell.
  • Optional Sunday, Reflection / essay. Long-form thought piece. Lower frequency.

Voice Rules

  • Achieve with Grace register, structured and calm, no hype
  • No banned AI slop terms (next-level, paradigm shift, harness the power, elevate your, seamlessly, effortlessly, game-changer, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, transform your life, unlock your potential, revolutionary)
  • No em dashes, comma rhythm + short paragraphs do the same work
  • 1-on-1 voice. "You" in singular, not "all of you"
  • Hospitality language welcome ("guest," "service," "operation," "cadence," "rhythm," "discipline")

Proof-Point Cadence

  • One hotel-scaling specific story per fortnight (the asymmetric credibility asset)
  • One client win per fortnight (anonymised where required)
  • One operator-tactic post per fortnight (installable on a Monday)
