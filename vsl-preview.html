Phase 1

VSL Preview Script · Theresa Lambert

Prepared 2026-05-11 by AG1 Consulting. Short preview only (3-5 min outline). Hero VSL for the consolidated homepage, with notes for the program-specific cutdowns.

Tone: hospitality-elegance, structured authority, calm under pressure. Achieve with Grace book voice. The opposite of yelling-at-camera.

Set: warm wood, soft natural light, structured composition. Whistler backdrop optional but on-brand. Wardrobe: blazer or structured knit, neutral palette.

Hero VSL · Consolidated Homepage (4-7 min target, this preview lays out 3-5 min beats)

Beat 1 · Cold-open hook (0:00-0:30)

On-screen text: Before I called myself a coach.

Theresa (camera-direct):

"Before I called myself a coach or a consultant, I scaled a boutique hotel to 8-figures at the #1 ski resort in North America. I led a team that grew from 90 to 180 people. The operating cadence I built there is what I bring to every founder and operator I work with today."

Cut to: soft cutaway, Whistler exterior or hospitality interior B-roll.

Beat 2 · Operator credibility (0:30-1:30)

Theresa (camera-direct):

"What most people miss about hospitality is that the discipline is invisible from the front. The guest sees a calm lobby. They never see the Monday meeting that closed the previous week or the Friday review that set up the next one.

That invisible cadence is the difference between a property that scales and one that breaks the people who scale it.

When I built Achieve with Grace, the book I wrote in 2020, the centre of it was that exact distinction. Elegance and effectiveness in intense workplaces. Operators who scale with grace versus operators who scale through grinding."

Cut to: book cover overlay, podcast cover, Brainz column screenshot, brief lower-thirds list of credentials.

Beat 3 · Methodology bridge (1:30-3:00)

Theresa (camera-direct):

"The founders I work with are typically in one of two stages.

The first stage is the founder building the business. Coaches, consultants, agency owners, course creators on the path from a working offer to a consistent six figures and beyond. The work there is strategy and mentorship. Helping the founder build the operating cadence under the revenue, instead of chasing the revenue with effort.

The second stage is the operator scaling a business that is already built. The work there is Fractional COO and Systems. Embedded weekly cadence. Executive sparring. The hospitality-grade discipline applied to a business that has product-market fit and now needs the operating layer to scale without breaking.

Same methodology, different stage of engagement."

Cut to: quiet transition shot, possibly the hotel exterior or a warm interior frame.

Beat 4 · The two tracks (3:00-4:30)

Theresa (camera-direct):

"For a long time I ran those two surfaces in parallel. One website for the coaching side, one website for the consulting side.

This year I consolidated. One roof. Two clearly-positioned tracks.

The coaching track holds the signature framework, The Way to $100K, the cohort container, Your First 100k Mastermind, and the 1:1 high-touch mentorship, ASCENT.

The consulting track holds the Fractional COO and Systems engagement, by application only, for established operators ready to scale without the founder being the bottleneck.

Both tracks are an expression of the same operating philosophy. The difference is where you are now."

Cut to: simple two-column overlay: COACHING · CONSULTING. Hospitality-elegance typography.

Beat 5 · Invitation (4:30-5:00)

Theresa (camera-direct):

"If you want to figure out which track is for you, the 90-second route map is on this page. Three questions. One clear next step.

If you would rather start with the signature framework, The Way to $100K is below.

If you would rather have the conversation directly, you can apply for the Mastermind or the Fractional COO consulting. Both routes are application-only, and both routes are conversations, not pitches.

Either way, thank you for taking the time. The door is open."

End frame: Theresa name + Achieve with grace. tagline. Soft fade.

Production Notes

  • Length target: 4-5 minutes recorded for first install. Can extend to 7 minutes if a deep-cut version is desired (additional Beat between 3 and 4 covering the hotel-team-fraying story from Email 5).
  • Camera: single 4K static frame, eye-level, soft natural light. No handheld, no jump cuts. The calm composition is the point.
  • Audio: lavalier mic, room-treated audio. No background music or very soft instrumental ambient if any.
  • Lower thirds: Cormorant Garamond serif, teal #106478 accent, sparse usage. Only for credential anchors (book cover, podcast title, Brainz column) and the two-track overlay.
  • B-roll: Whistler hospitality interior + exterior, book cover, podcast cover. Held shots, not montage-edited.
  • Wardrobe: structured knit or blazer, neutral palette (cream, sage, navy, soft teal). On-brand with the hospitality-elegance register.
  • CTA overlay: appears in last 30 seconds. Buttons not animated, no urgency timers, no countdown.

Program-Specific VSL Cutdowns (2-3 min each)

  5. Application CTA

ASCENT VSL · 1:1 High-Touch Mentorship

Beats:

  2. ICP fit: founders already past the Mastermind stage, with the business built and ready for the next plateau
  3. What the engagement looks like (frequency, depth, deliverables)
  4. The hospitality-elegance posture of the 1:1 work
  5. Application CTA

Fractional COO VSL · Consulting Track

Beats:

  1. Cold-open: "Fractional COO is the embedded operator layer for businesses that are already built."
  2. ICP fit: $500K-$5M+ revenue, founder-led, systems-light
  3. What the engagement looks like (weekly cadence, embedded depth)
  4. The hospitality-grade discipline applied to B2B operating
  5. Application CTA

Hub VSL Treatment (Per Andrei's Explicit Ask)

The hub page (this prospect package's index.html) includes a small VSL section as a static video-poster block. No YouTube iframe (no verified videoId). The poster uses a CSS typographic composition with a play-button glyph that scrolls to the Calendly CTA on click. Same treatment as the landing page's hero VSL block.

This is an explicit override of the standard "no video on hub" rule, applied here because Andrei wants VSL on every page in this package.

What This VSL Will Not Do

  • It will not yell, manufacture urgency, or use countdown timers
  • It will not stack triple adjectives
  • It will not claim "life-changing," "transformational," or anything in the banned AI-slop set
  • It will not run longer than 7 minutes (lower retention past that point on this register)
  • It will not be talking-head only, minimal but intentional B-roll keeps the hospitality-elegance frame intact

  • Embed on the diagnostic quiz exit page (right-tier confirmation)

  • YouTube unlisted full-length (for embed reliability) + a public 60-second teaser
