6. Brand Voice Notes

The "Achieve with Grace" book voice is the through-line. Translation for copy:

  • Register: structured, professional, hospitality-elegance. Calm authority, not hype. The opposite of the Instagram coach yelling-at-camera energy.
  • Vocabulary in her own copy: "elegance," "effectiveness," "high-level," "individualized," "strategic," "scale," "growth without overwhelm," "intentional," "grace under pressure" (book title), "service-based entrepreneurs," "coaches," "founders."
  • Sentence shape: longer than coaching-bro average. Structured clauses. Comma rhythm. Reads more like a book than a sales page.
  • Avoid (matching her voice): hype words, slang, all-caps urgency, manufactured scarcity, fitness-bro brevity. Also avoid the AG1 universal banned-phrase list (see copy-strategy.md). Theresa's brand uses some adjacent professional language ("achieve," "elegance," "effective"), preserve those, do not echo the banned set.
  • CTA register: invitation, not pressure. "Explore," "discover," "book a call," "apply." Not "claim your spot now."
