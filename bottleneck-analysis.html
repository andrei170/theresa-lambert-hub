Bottleneck Analysis · Theresa Lambert

Prepared 2026-05-11 by AG1 Consulting. Five bottlenecks ranked by revenue impact. Four Install bottlenecks (one-time funnel architecture) + one Recurring Engagement bottleneck (LinkedIn long-form ghostwriting cadence).

Sources: theresalambertconsulting.com, theresalambertcoaching.com, LinkedIn (linkedin.com/in/theresa-lambert), Instagram (@theresalambertconsulting), Brainz contributor archive, "Diaries of a Six Figure Coach" podcast feed, "Achieve with Grace" book.

Bottleneck 1 · Two Domains Routing The Same Theresa, No Bridge

Highest Revenue Lever · Install.

Snapshot

Same founder, same proven methodology, two completely separate domains:

  • theresalambertconsulting.com, "High-level, individualized Systems Support + Strategies for Business Owners ready to scale and 10x their growth without 10x their workload." Fractional COO / Systems / Strategic Consulting register. B2B leaning.
  • theresalambertcoaching.com, "Strategy + Mentorship for service-based Entrepreneurs + Coaches." Coaching / mentorship register. Service-business leaning.

Two domains. Two separate brand identities. Two separate funnels. ICPs overlap heavily (female founders / coaches / agency owners often need BOTH operational systems AND strategic mentorship at different stages) but there is no consolidated front door.

Asymmetry

Cold traffic hits one OR the other and has no idea they overlap. A prospect who needs operational systems first and then strategic mentorship six months later sees Theresa twice in two unrelated registers and treats them as two separate decisions. SEO juice splits across two domains for the same person. Email lists fragment. Brand recall halves.

Why it's costing her

  • Decision tax. Cold prospect can't tell which surface is for them. Bounces.
  • SEO dilution. Both domains compete for "Theresa Lambert" + adjacent queries. Backlinks fragment. Domain authority spreads thin across two URLs that should be one.
  • Brand split. Same hospitality-elegance founder identity gets presented in two different registers. Cold prospect remembers "I saw a coach called Theresa" or "I saw a consultant called Theresa" but doesn't connect them.
  • Cross-sell dead. Coaching graduates who'd benefit from Fractional COO at the next stage don't know the consulting surface exists. Consulting clients who'd benefit from the Mastermind don't know the coaching surface exists.

The fix

ONE umbrella domain. Likely consolidate to theresalambertcoaching.com (most established surface in search) or move both under a unified theresalambert.com if Andrei thinks the domain swap is worth it. Decision made Day 1 of install.

Under the umbrella, two clearly-positioned tracks:

  1. COACHING, Strategy + Mentorship for service-based founders (existing eight programs, organised into a visible ladder per Bottleneck 2)
  2. CONSULTING, Fractional COO + Systems for established operators (existing 1:1 consulting offer, packaged with a visible tier ladder)

Shared founder block, shared lead magnet, shared email nurture sequence routing into the right track, shared SEO. Cold-traffic decision tax eliminated.

Estimated impact

Brand consolidation conservatively recovers 30-50% of cold-traffic drop-off currently leaking across the two domains. Year-1 floor: $40K-$120K incremental program enrollments + retained SEO traffic + recovered cross-sell between the two registers.

Why this is #1

Every other bottleneck downstream compounds off this fix. The offer ladder (Bottleneck 2) doesn't matter if cold traffic can't decide which front door they belong on. The VSL (Bottleneck 3) gets installed twice if she has two homepages. The LinkedIn ghostwriting (Bottleneck 4) splits authority across two founder identities. Fix the front door first.

Bottleneck 2 · Eight+ Named Programs, No Visible $-Tier Ladder

Fast Install · Install.

Snapshot

On the coaching surface alone:

  • Your First 100k Mastermind
  • 1:1 ASCENT
  • The 5-fig Launch Method Masterclass
  • LAUNCHED
  • DROOLWORTHY CONTENT 101
  • IRRESISTIBLE OFFERS
  • The Way to $100K
  • Tulum Retreat

Plus Fractional COO consulting on the other surface. Nine total offers across two domains. No visible $entry → $mid → $high ladder. No diagnostic that routes a cold prospect to the right one. Most prospects bounce because the choice is too wide.

Asymmetry

Same Theresa with the credibility to charge across all nine offers. But the cold prospect doesn't know which to start with, which costs what, or what the path through them looks like. Every offer competes for attention with every other offer on the same page. Internal cannibalisation: the $497 masterclass and the $5K Mastermind sit next to each other and neither converts.

Why it's costing her

  • Choice overload. Cold prospect can't pick. Bounces or asks "which one is for me?", sales cycle slows.
  • No clear path. Even prospects who do buy the entry offer don't know what comes next. No ladder, no upsell, no retention layer.
  • Pricing opacity. Prospects can't tell which programs are $97 vs $5K. The high-ticket programs likely under-convert because the entry-tier reader assumes "this is all in the same range" and bounces.

The fix

Visible 3-tier offer ladder, mapped against the existing programs:

  • Entry · $97-$497, ONE signature product. Likely The Way to $100K (already positioned as a signature framework) or a repackaged masterclass version of The 5-fig Launch Method. Public price visible.
  • Mid · $1.5K-$5K, ONE mastermind / cohort. Your First 100k Mastermind slots here cleanly. LAUNCHED / DROOLWORTHY CONTENT 101 / IRRESISTIBLE OFFERS fold into the Mastermind curriculum as modules rather than competing standalone products.
  • High · $5K-$25K, ONE 1:1 offer per track. 1:1 ASCENT (coaching track) + Fractional COO Consulting (consulting track). Application-only, price confirmed at intake.
  • Event · ad-hoc, Tulum Retreat stays as a stand-alone yearly event, sold to the existing ladder.

A 3-question diagnostic quiz on the consolidated homepage routes prospects: "Where are you now?" → entry / mid / high.

Estimated impact

Offer-ladder conversion typically lifts 2-4x on warm-traffic mid-tier products once the path is visible. Year-1 floor: $30K-$80K incremental mid-tier enrollments from the same traffic she's already attracting + reduced sales-cycle drag on the high-tier.

Why this is #2

Compounds directly off Bottleneck 1. Once the front door is one domain, the ladder organises everything behind it. Fast install (4-7 days post-consolidation).

Bottleneck 3 · No Flagship VSL Pre-Framing The Methodology

Compound Pickup · Install.

Snapshot

Theresa has the rarest credibility cocktail in the coaching space: scaled a boutique hotel to 8-figures at the #1 ski resort in North America, led a team of 90 to 180+ people, Bestselling Author of "Achieve with Grace," host of "Diaries of a Six Figure Coach" podcast, Brainz Magazine Executive Contributor, Certified Professional Coach.

Both her sites are text-and-image driven. There is no hero video walking a cold prospect through the methodology in 4-7 minutes on either surface.

Asymmetry

The hotel-scaling story is the single most compelling proof asset in her portfolio. Most coaches' bios read "I'm a coach who coaches." Theresa's reads "I scaled a boutique hotel to 8-figures at the #1 ski resort in North America before I ever called myself a coach." That story, told as a 4-7 minute hero VSL, would carry a meaningful share of the cold-conversion lift on its own.

She's not telling it on the homepage. Cold prospects who land on text-and-image sites bounce at higher rates than cold prospects who watch the founder explain why she's qualified.

Why it's costing her

  • Cold-conversion floor. Without VSL, text-only sites typically convert cold traffic at 1-2%. With a hero VSL, the floor moves to 3-5% on coaching/consulting traffic of this register.
  • Hot Girl proof under-deployed. The hotel-scaling story is doing 10% of the work it could do because it lives in the bio text instead of as the first thing a cold prospect hears in her voice.
  • Per-page conversion. Each program landing page (Mastermind, ASCENT, Fractional COO) should have its own VSL pre-framing that specific offer. Currently none do.

The fix

  • Hero VSL · consolidated homepage. 4-7 minutes. Hospitality-elegance register, matching the Achieve with Grace book voice. Beats: hotel-scaling story → Achieve with Grace methodology → consolidated coaching + consulting offer → entry-tier CTA. Recorded once, lives forever.
  • Program-specific VSLs. A shorter VSL (2-3 min) on each program landing: Mastermind landing, ASCENT landing, Fractional COO landing. Same hero footage can be recut into the program-specific versions on Day 1, then re-shot to bespoke versions on Day 14-21.
  • Andrei's explicit ask: VSL on EACH page, including the hub itself (static video poster, no live YouTube embed without a verified videoId).

Estimated impact

VSL typically lifts cold-traffic conversion from 1-2% to 3-5% on warm coaching/consulting traffic. Year-1 floor: $40K-$120K of recovered revenue from the same traffic + retention lift on the program pages as warm prospects pre-framed by the VSL convert at higher rates.

Why this is #3

Compounds off Bottlenecks 1 and 2. The hero VSL needs ONE homepage to live on (Bottleneck 1) and a clear offer ladder to point at (Bottleneck 2). Once both upstream fixes are in, the VSL install is a recording + embed task.

Bottleneck #5 (Install): Book + Podcast Have No Nurture Funnel Into Paid Programs

Snapshot. "Achieve with Grace" launched 2020, five-plus years in market, Bestselling Author status, regular Amazon traffic. "Diaries of a Six Figure Coach" Podcast publishes new episodes regularly on Apple and Spotify. Both audiences are warmer than any cold ad traffic, they have already chosen to invest four-plus hours with Theresa's voice (book read time) or thirty-plus minutes per episode (podcast listening). And yet: book buyers route to Amazon and disappear. Podcast listeners route to Apple and Spotify and the relationship ends there. There is no observable email capture, no chapter-mapped nurture, no podcast-to-program funnel.

Asymmetry. Industry norm: book buyers convert to high-ticket coaching at two to five percent with a competent nurture sequence (free chapter download, fourteen-day chapter-mapped email sequence, tier-routed CTA). Podcast listeners convert at half to one percent with a similar nurture (episode-mapped opt-in, companion content, tier-routed CTA). At Theresa's stage and audience density, the warm-segment funnel is the highest-leverage install because the cost of content acquisition is already paid, only the routing is missing.

Why it's costing her. Every book sale and every podcast listen that does not capture the email is a permanent loss of warm-audience inventory. Five years of book sales plus several seasons of podcast episodes equals thousands of warm-audience moments that did not compound. The book and the podcast become content investments that "build the brand" instead of content investments that "feed the funnel."

The fix. Two-part nurture install:

  1. Book funnel: free chapter download landing page, fourteen-day chapter-mapped email sequence anchored on the Achieve with Grace framework, tier-routed CTA (entry program for early-stage readers, mastermind for mid-stage, 1:1 ASCENT for ready-to-scale founders, Fractional COO for established operators).
  2. Podcast funnel: each episode published with a companion landing page (three-to-four minute pre-frame video plus episode-specific opt-in), eight-touch nurture sequence pulling from the book and the episode catalogue, same tier-routed CTA.

Estimated impact. $40K-$160K Year-1 floor on the warm-audience funnel install. At her stage (Bestselling Book, active podcast, eight-figure hotel credibility), the only piece missing is the email capture infrastructure to convert warm audience into paid clients.

Why this is #5 (and the lowest-friction Install lift). Brand consolidation (#1) requires merging two domains. Program ladder (#2) requires re-designing the comparison structure. VSL (#3) requires recording new video. Book and podcast nurture (#5) is the install with the least new content production required, the book is written, the podcast episodes exist. Only the email sequences and the landing pages need building. Highest-leverage, lowest-friction.

Bottleneck 4 · LinkedIn Long-Form Cadence Under-Built For B2B Capture

Recurring Engagement · Ongoing.

Snapshot

Theresa's strongest channel is LinkedIn. Her ICP is exec / female-founder / Fractional COO B2B, all LinkedIn-native. She posts consistently on the feed but the long-form thought-leadership cadence (the kind that pulls $5K-$25K Fractional COO leads inbound) is room to lift, not currently the centre of gravity.

YouTube long-form is underweight. The podcast catalogue (audio) is not currently being repurposed into YouTube long-form video, LinkedIn carousel essays, or B2B exec-coaching positioning posts at the cadence the audience would absorb.

Asymmetry

Same audience-yes / distribution-no pattern that has played out for other operators with strong owned-channel presence and underweight long-form repurposing: the audience is reachable, the content already exists in the podcast feed, but the distribution layer that turns that content into Fractional COO + exec-coaching B2B leads doesn't yet exist at the cadence that compounds.

Why it's costing her

  • B2B leads under-captured. A Fractional COO engagement is worth $20K-$50K. Each missed inbound on LinkedIn is one of those engagements walking past.
  • Authority compounds against her. Competing exec coaches without her credibility stack are out-publishing her on LinkedIn. Over 12 months, the gap compounds.
  • Podcast catalogue is sunk cost. The episodes already exist. Not repurposing them into LinkedIn long-form + YouTube long-form is leaving authority assets in audio-only format.

The fix

A monthly ongoing engagement on LinkedIn long-form ghostwriting (4-5x/week, Fractional COO leader-positioning cadence in her voice, Achieve with Grace book voice as the through-line) + YouTube long-form repurpose of the podcast catalogue (1-2x/week, 8-fig hotel scaling case studies + executive-coaching breakdowns).

Recent Install proof: our best client went from $0 to $172k/mo in our first month working together, closed-door B2B funnel architecture work. Same methodology of fixing the structural gaps before adding traffic applies directly to the Theresa Lambert brand consolidation.

This is framed as ongoing monthly engagement, not retainer language. No dollar figures attached on AG1's side at this stage.

Estimated impact

Conservative Year-1 floor: $60K-$300K from LinkedIn-sourced B2B exec coaching + Fractional COO B2B leads at $20K-$50K each, compounding monthly as the LinkedIn audience grows.

Why this is #4

Independent of Bottlenecks 1-3 but compounds with them. The Install fixes (1-3) build the funnel; the Recurring Engagement (4) feeds the top of it.

Combined Year-1 Floor

  • Install (Bottlenecks 1-3 + 5): $150K-$480K incremental
  • Recurring Engagement (Bottleneck 4): $60K-$300K
  • Combined ceiling: $210K-$780K Year-1.

Numbers are conservative floors based on the public surfaces visible at research time. Real numbers swap in once we walk through the consolidated funnel together.
